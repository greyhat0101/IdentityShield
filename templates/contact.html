Frequently Asked Questions
How does the email scanning work?
We send your email to the XposedOrNot API which cross-references it against 10B+ breach records from 500+ known data breaches. No password is ever required or stored.
Is the service completely free?
Yes! IdentityShield is 100% free to use. You can scan unlimited emails, view your dashboard, and access all cybersecurity awareness content without any payment.
How secure is my data?
We take privacy seriously. Your scanned emails are only used to query the breach database and are stored only in your account's scan history. We never sell or share your data.
What should I do if breaches are found?
Immediately change the password for all affected accounts, enable Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA), check your login activity for suspicious sessions, and consider using a password manager.
How often should I scan my email?
We recommend scanning at least once a month, or whenever you hear about a major data breach in the news. New breaches are discovered regularly, so regular monitoring is important.
